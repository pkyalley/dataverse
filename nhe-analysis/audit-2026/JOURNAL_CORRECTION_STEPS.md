# Steps for contacting World Journal of Advanced Research and Reviews

Article DOI: 10.30574/wjarr.2026.29.1.0020.

1. Run `reproduce.py` and preserve results.
2. Independently reproduce Table 02/03/07 and all published charts, checking exact article pages, captions, methods, dates and references.
3. Prepare a single correction matrix with original and corrected text, figure/table location, evidence and implications.
4. Confirm agreement of any coauthors.
5. Contact the journal using the contact details verified on its official site. Ask about erratum/corrigendum procedure, charges, whether requests must be consolidated, and publication linkage.
6. Submit a single source-verified package only after review. Keep original public record and code intact.
7. Verify published correction and DOI linkage before describing the article as corrected.
