# HAEA — Healthcare Administrative Efficiency Analytics

**Independent research project.** Started 2026-10-14. This project is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or based on data from Rochester Regional Health (RRH).

## Purpose
Investigate measurable patterns of healthcare administrative spending, utilization, and process efficiency using reproducible public data. Explore referral/documentation quality through a **separate synthetic-data methods demonstration**, never misrepresented as observed clinical results.

## Workstreams
1. **Public-data empirical study:** establish constructs and available measures; test descriptive trends and carefully specified associations using documented CMS/AHRQ datasets.
2. **Synthetic referral/documentation prototype:** demonstrate validation rules, defect categories, queue and rework metrics on entirely fabricated records; evaluate engineering performance, not real-world prevalence or effects.

## Research integrity
- No RRH patient, staff, operational, proprietary, internal workflow, or other employer-derived data or documents.
- No real patient identifiers; no production system access; no employer sponsorship implied.
- No unlicensed data rehosting. Track dataset license, source, version, extraction date, dictionary, transformations, exclusions.
- Separate observed results, simulated results, and hypothetical scenarios in all outputs.
- Do not carry disputed values from the 2026 NHE paper into new analysis; independently verify every claim.
- No causal claim from correlations or before/after comparisons without an appropriate identification strategy.
- Check source terms and any IRB/human-subjects requirements before changing the study to include real people or nonpublic records.

## Current stage
**Phase 1 — protocol and data feasibility.** No empirical results or claims of demonstrated savings yet.
